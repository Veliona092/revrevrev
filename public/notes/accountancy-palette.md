# Accountancy Side – Color Palette Reasoning

## Sidebar – Dark Espresso `#2C2416`
- Warm dark brown, not plain black — avoids eye strain
- Evokes a professional, financial feel (think leather, mahogany)
- Distinct from teacher's green and education's indigo

## Active Bar – Gold/Amber `#C17F24`
- Gold is universally associated with finance and value
- Stands out clearly against the dark espresso sidebar

## Content Background – Warm Cream `#F7F3EC`
- Warm and easy on the eyes, different from education's lavender tint
- Slightly richer than Seashell, more premium feel

## Topbar – `#FBF8F3`
- Lighter than the content bg to visually separate the header
- Stays in the warm cream family, no pure white

## Primary Button – Gold `#C17F24`
- Consistent with the active bar accent — ties the theme together
- Strong enough to draw attention as a call-to-action

## Success – Tea Green `#245E55`
- Consistent across all roles for predictable UX

## Danger – Red Passion `#C63F3E`
- Consistent across all roles for predictable UX

## Inputs – `#FBF8F3` bg + Gold focus `#C17F24`
- Warm background matches the topbar so forms feel native
- Gold focus ring reinforces the accountancy theme color
