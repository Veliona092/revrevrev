# Education Side – Color Palette Reasoning

## Sidebar – Deep Indigo `#3A4180`
- Distinct from teacher's Tea Green — each role needs its own identity
- Derived from Lavender in Image 3, darkened to be readable as a sidebar
- Calm and intellectual, fits a student learning context

## Active Bar – Sky `#9ED6DF`
- Light, airy contrast against the dark indigo sidebar
- From Image 3, pairs naturally with the Lavender family

## Content Background – Light Lavender `#F0EEF8`
- Tinted version of the Lavender theme color
- Warmer than plain grey, easier on the eyes during long study sessions

## Topbar – `#F8F7FC`
- Slightly lighter than the content bg so the topbar feels separated
- Still in the lavender family, no pure white

## Primary Button – Lavender `#808BC5`
- The core accent color of the education theme
- Soft enough to not feel aggressive, still clearly clickable

## Success – Tea Green `#245E55`
- Kept consistent with the teacher side
- Green universally signals positive/confirm — no reason to change per role

## Danger – Red Passion `#C63F3E`
- Same as teacher side for consistency
- Muted red, serious without being alarming

## Inputs – `#F8F7FC` bg + Lavender focus `#808BC5`
- Matches the topbar so forms feel native to the layout
- Focus ring ties back to the primary accent color
