# Teacher Side – Color Palette Reasoning

## Sidebar – Tea Green `#245E55`
- Not plain black/white, easier on the eyes
- Deep teal reads as calm and academic
- Distinct from other role dashboards in the system

## Active Bar – Mustard Yellow `#EAC119`
- High contrast against the tea green sidebar
- Warm and energetic, stands out without being aggressive

## Content Background – Seashell `#EAE4DA`
- Warm off-white, avoids the harshness of pure white
- Easy to read text on top of it for long sessions

## Topbar – `#F7F2E9`
- Slightly lighter than Seashell so the topbar feels separated
- Still warm, no pure white

## Primary Button – Tangerine `#ED773C`
- Warm orange draws the eye to main actions
- Works well on both dark and light backgrounds

## Success Button – Tea Green `#245E55`
- Reuses sidebar color for consistency
- Green naturally signals positive/confirm actions

## Danger – Red Passion `#C63F3E`
- Muted red, not neon — serious without being alarming
- From the same named palette, keeps things cohesive

## Inputs – Seashell bg + Tea focus `#245E55`
- Matches the page background so forms feel native
- Tea focus ring ties back to the sidebar accent
