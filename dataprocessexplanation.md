Figure 8 shows the Level 1 process model of Reviso. It provides step by step of the workflow. it shows creating an account to reviewing learning performance. The process starts with a student submitting a signup form. The system accepts the signup as pending and sends a verification email. Admins manage approvals, users, and AI settings; teachers create and manage classes, enroll students, post announcements, and prepare modules or assessments. Students access their assigned dashboard based on their course and join class activities. Students then access module content. Document modules are progress-tracked as students read them. For assessments, the system checks whether an attempt is currently available, saves each answer and attempt, computes score and percentage, updates module progress, and tracks attempt count. When needed, teachers can reset a student attempt to allow another try in the supported flow. Teachers can also generate AI insights for students or class performance, with generated results cached and reused until refreshed. Assessment records are available for teacher review. Teachers review combined signals from student progress, quiz results, and AI insights. The Level-1 model shows how processes, roles, and decisions connect, turning user actions into measurable learning outcomes and useful feedback.
